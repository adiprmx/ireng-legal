Section 01

Why this document exists

Most privacy policies are written to tick a box, not to be read. This one is different: Ireng is a community bot, and a community runs on trust.

This document honestly lists what data Ireng stores while you hang out in the ADIP RMX server, what it's used for, who it's shared with, and how long it's kept. No legalese hiding the important parts, no tiny asterisks at the bottom. If anything here makes you uncomfortable after reading it, that's your right — and how to speak up is in Section 07.

Section 02

What Ireng stores

One principle: store as little as possible — only what the features genuinely need. This is the complete list; nothing is hidden outside this table.

DataWhat's in itWhat's it for
Discord identityUsername, user ID, avatarShowing member cards, levels, and personal greetings
Levels & XPChat & voice activity pointsThe server level system and achievement badges

What's not stored: the contents of your DMs, any password or token of yours, and your browsing history. Ireng has no business with any of that.

03
Section 03

Private rooms: your stronghold

Private rooms (/ruangpribadi) are where you chat with Ireng's AI — and this is the part guarded most seriously.

Your chats in your private room are your business. The bot won't serve anyone else who wanders in, won't keep snippets of the conversation, and won't show it to anyone.

— Ireng's design promise
  • Private-room channels are locked to their owner: other members and regular staff can't see them from the channel view.
  • Ireng only talks to the registered room owner. Anyone else typing there is ignored completely — no response, no trace.
  • Chats the bot fails to answer in a private room are not entered into any error log the owner can read.

Being upfront: the server owner and Administrator permission holders can technically still open any channel — that's a Discord platform limitation, not Ireng's choice. But no staff or member can, and the bot won't serve them.

04
Section 04

What it's all for

In short: to keep the server running and fun. Nothing more.

All data in Section 02 is used only to run the bot's features in the ADIP RMX server — levels, member cards, moderation, backups, and AI chat. Data is never sold, never shared with third parties for commercial purposes, and never used for advertising. Full stop.

05
Section 05

Third parties & AI

This part is written out explicitly so nobody feels misled.

When you chat in a private room, your message is forwarded to an AI model provider (Google Gemini as the primary, with backup providers if the primary is at capacity) to generate a reply. AI providers process the message content and the sender's username under their own respective policies. This is the only "third party" besides Discord itself as the platform this bot runs on — and the processing is purely for the chat feature, never for selling or advertising.

06
Section 06

Security

No system is 100% unbreakable — all you can do is make breaking in as hard as possible.

  • Data access is restricted — only the bot system and the server owner.
  • Bot credentials are stored in a local configuration file with restricted file permissions (chmod 600), and are never shown in chat.
  • No system is 100% unbreakable — if an incident ever involves user data, it will be announced in the server's announcement channel.

Section 07

Your rights

The data is about you — so you're the one in control.

  • See it — request a summary of the data the bot stores about you.
  • Delete it — request permanent deletion of your data.
  • Leave — leaving the server = Ireng stops collecting new data about you.

How: contact the owner / staff of ADIP RMX. Reasonable requests will be followed up.
