Section 01

By playing, you agree

Nobody reads terms to the end — but the gist is one thing: using Ireng means agreeing to the house rules on this page.

By running Ireng commands, chatting in a private room, or simply being a member of the ADIP RMX server, you're considered to have read and agreed to these terms, plus the Privacy Policy and the server rules. Don't agree? The solution is simple: don't use the service.

You must also meet Discord's minimum age requirement and comply with the Discord Terms of Service.

Section 02

What is Ireng, exactly

A community bot for ADIP RMX: keeps the peace, up for a chat, and keeps the server alive. Here's its job list.

  • Automatic & manual moderation (anti-spam, anti-gambling, anti-toxic, anti-raid).
  • AI chat in private rooms via /ruangpribadi.
  • Level/XP system, member cards, and achievements.
  • Community features: ft partner matching, suggestions, events, and more.

Features may change, grow, or be retired at any time as the server evolves. This server is alive, and its bot lives with it.

03
Section 03

The house rules

Not complicated: don't wreck things, don't abuse features, don't pretend to be someone else.

  • No bypassing the bot's limits: command spam, bug exploits, or gaming cooldowns.
  • No abusing automated features (auto-responder, reports, suggestions) to bother other people.
  • No spreading illegal content, gambling, or hateful content, or content that breaks server & Discord rules through bot features.
  • No impersonating staff/the owner, or trying to access commands that aren't yours. Moderation & configuration commands are staff-only — full stop.
  • No buying or selling access, badges, or data related to the bot.

💡 Found a bug? Report it to staff — don't exploit it. People who report honestly usually get appreciated instead.

Section 04

Private rooms

Your room, your rules — within reason.

  • One user = one private room. The room belongs to its owner.
  • Don't share access, force your way in, or snoop on other people's rooms.
  • What you chat about is your own responsibility — server rules still apply in there.
  • Rooms can be deleted by the owner/staff if they're misused or break the rules.

Section 05

The AI can be wrong

Ireng is smart, but not psychic. Don't bet important decisions on a bot's answer.

AI answers are generated automatically and can simply be wrong, off the rails, or incomplete — that's their nature, not a bug.

— A fact worth holding onto

Don't use it as your only reference for important decisions (health, legal, financial, etc.). The ADIP RMX team isn't responsible for decisions made purely from the bot's answers.

Section 06

If you misbehave

The system is gradual — one mistake doesn't get you thrown out. But there are limits.

Violating these terms or the server rules can lead to: warning → timeout → kick → ban, depending on the severity. The warning system runs in stages and is recorded; staff decisions are final. If you believe an action taken against you was mistaken, use the appeal channel the server provides.

Section 07

Work & logos

Your work is yours. The server's logo is the server's. As simple as that.

The ADIP RMX and Ireng logos, names, and visual identity belong to the server owner — don't reuse them outside the server without permission. Work you share in the server (remixes, artwork, etc.) stays yours; you only grant permission for it to be displayed inside the server.

Ireng is kept online around the clock as best we can, but this is a free community service — not a paid one with an SLA.

The service is provided "as is": downtime can happen for maintenance, updates, or things beyond our control (host, Discord, or internet issues). There's no 100% uptime guarantee, and we're not liable for losses caused by service disruptions or lost progress (XP/badges) due to technical incidents.
